# blender-handControl-AR — Dotrealm-dev

- **What it is:** Real-time hand-gesture control of the Blender viewport camera via phone camera (MJPEG stream) + MediaPipe; orbit/pan/zoom/reset with natural gestures over WiFi.

- **Fits stage:** User-driven camera control (gesture variant).
